Privacy

No ads. No analytics. No account.

There is no business model here that needs your data.

  • No data collection.

    The app’s privacy manifest declares nothing collected and nothing tracked.

  • Your library stays with you.

    Favorites and recents live on your device. No server, no sync service, no sign-up.

  • Only the traffic radio needs.

    Directory queries, the streams you play, album-art lookups with Apple’s iTunes Search, and anonymous play counts that help the community directory rank stations.

  • This page, too.

    No CDN fonts, no analytics, no cookies. One HTML file.

Build it in three commands

No keys, no secrets, no sign-up. Just Xcode 27 and a Mac.

git clone https://github.com/cascadiacollections/shoutkit.git
cd shoutkit
open Holmdel.xcworkspace # then ⌘R

Prefer the terminal? swift test --disable-xctest runs it without a simulator.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases. The app is open source, and every feature works for everyone.

Why would I pay for a store version?

You don’t have to. App Store pricing hasn’t been decided. If a store version ever costs something, you’d be paying for the convenience of installing and updating through the store, and helping fund development, not unlocking features. The source stays open, so you can always build it yourself.

Does it track me?

No. No ads, no analytics, no account, and the privacy manifest declares no collected data and no tracking. The app talks only to what radio needs: the station directory, the streams you play, Apple’s iTunes Search for album art (it sends the current artist and track title), and anonymous play counts that help the directory rank stations.

Which stations can I listen to?

Thousands. Stations come from Radio-Browser, a free, community-run directory that anyone can add to or correct, so no API key or account is needed.

Which devices does it run on?

The beta runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26 and later, with Apple Watch, Apple TV and CarPlay support. The Mac Catalyst preview builds from source. On Android, see Shoutkit for Android.

Why does the beta say Holmdel or Squan?

The app is Shoutkit. While it’s in beta, TestFlight lists it as Squan and it installs on your Home Screen as Holmdel. It’s the same app.

Can I build it myself?

Yes. Clone it, open Holmdel.xcworkspace in Xcode 27, and run it. No secrets needed.
