Internet radio,

ShoutKit is a native iOS internet-radio client with an Apple Music–style Liquid Glass player, keyless station discovery, and live track metadata — with zero tracking and zero accounts.

View on GitHub Explore features

Everything you'd expect, natively built

A modern SwiftUI app that feels at home on iOS — not a web wrapper.

🎛️

Liquid Glass player

A persistent mini-player docked above the tab bar, plus a full-screen Now Playing surface with live ICY track metadata.

🌐

Keyless discovery

Real station discovery out of the box via Radio-Browser's community directory — search, genres, and popular stations with no API key.

🔒

Lock screen & Control Center

Full remote controls and now-playing info on the lock screen and in Control Center, with a Dynamic Island Live Activity.

🗣️

Siri, Shortcuts & Spotlight

"Play KEXP on ShoutKit." App Intents power headless background playback — no app foregrounding required — and favorited or recently-played stations are indexed in Spotlight for semantic search.

⭐

Favorites & recents

Your favorites and listening history are backed by SwiftData and stored locally on your device. Drag to reorder favorites into any arrangement you like.

🎵

Album art & Apple Music

Now Playing resolves album artwork from ICY track metadata and links straight to the matching song in Apple Music — on by default, one Settings toggle turns it off.

📱

Built for iPad

An adaptive sidebar and multi-column station grids make ShoutKit feel native in full-screen iPad, Split View, and Stage Manager — not a stretched-out iPhone layout.

🚗

CarPlay

Your favorites and recent stations on the car's screen, with the system now-playing controls — driven by the same playback engine as the phone, so what's playing stays in sync.

⌚️

Apple Watch

A native watch app for now playing and recent stations, plus a one-tap "Play Last" complication that starts your last station straight from the watch face.

🎛️

Equalizer & sleep timer

Preset EQ curves to colour the sound how you like it, and a sleep timer that pauses rather than stops — so resuming in the morning is one tap.

🔗

Deep links & widgets

A Home Screen widget plays a chosen favorite in one tap, and shoutkit://station?… deep links open the app straight to a station.

Private by design

ShoutKit collects nothing and tracks nothing. No analytics, no ads, no accounts.

✓
Zero data collection. The privacy manifest declares no collected data types and no tracking.
✓
No paywalls. Everything works whether or not you support the project.
✓
Local storage only. Favorites and recents live in a local SwiftData store — never uploaded.
✓
Minimal network traffic. Only public directory queries, station streams, and anonymous play reports the community directory uses to rank popularity.
